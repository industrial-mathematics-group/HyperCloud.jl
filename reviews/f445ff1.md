## Status of F1–F7

- **F1 — fixed.** Derivation (2-D and vector form): for an irrotational lab-frame flow `∇u` is symmetric, so
  `∂u_t/∂n = P_t(∇u n) = P_t ∇(u·n) − H u`; along the wall `u·n = w·n` with `w = v + Ω(x − c)` gives
  `∂_t(w·n) = (Ωᵀn)·t + w·H t`, hence `∂u_t/∂n = P_t(Ωᵀn) − H (u − w)_t` (`H n = 0`, H symmetric). The ghost is at
  `+d` along the outward normal and the mirror point at `−d`, so `u_t' = u_t + 2d ∂u_t/∂n` — exactly the code
  (`dut = −2d (H u_t − P_t(Ωᵀn))`, then `w + u_t + dut − u_n n`). Signs agree with Core's convention (t·Ht = +1/r inside
  a circle). Galilean invariant (only `u − w`, Ω, H enter); the normal momentum term `dpdn` already used `u − w`.
  Verified at operator level for static, translating, spinning about the centre, **rotating about an off-centre axis**
  (where the spin term is O(1) and does not cancel), 2-D and 3-D (sphere, cylinder with oblique translation): order 2 in
  velocity and pressure everywhere. In the runs: X4 0.12 → 6e-14, B16 back to the parent level (5.3e-2 / 3.0e-2), B7c
  1e-15, B7a/b pass. The early return `iszero(dpdn) && iszero(dut)` is exact for flat static and uniformly translating
  walls (all terms are signed zeros), and it is not a switch: as `dpdn, dut → 0` the general branch tends to the same
  state (`p2 → p`, `ρ2 → ρ`, `w + u_t − u_n n = u − 2u_n n`); X4 round-off cases agree to 3e-16 (F64) / 1.7e-7 (F32)
  whichever branch is taken (`probe_bounds.out`). The B7b surface-pressure gap (0.627 vs 0.650 at 400²) is unchanged
  from prev, so rr6's attribution of it to the lab-frame correction was wrong; it is not tied to these branches.
- **F2 — fixed for positivity, with a remaining issue in the tangential part (N2) and less margin than the parent
  (N4).** `p exp(clamp(2d ∂p/∂n / p, ±1/2))` matches `p + 2d ∂p/∂n` to O((2d ∂p/∂n/p)²), which is the same order as the
  linear form's truncation error, so second order is kept while the clamp is inactive; always positive; ρ isentropic
  follows smoothly. The clamp is inactive in the smooth tests: B13 has `2d γM²/r ≤ 2·0.1·0.7 = 0.14` even at N = 50,
  B4b `2d ρ|a|/p ≈ 1e-6`; B4b is unchanged (0.18 %), B13 moves only by the log-vs-linear and the sqrt-vs-linear
  tangential difference (L1(ρ) 2.1e-4 vs 2.0e-4 at 200², near-wall velocity 2.2× better). X5 is positive at every
  resolution, both methods, also in Float32. Shocks at curved walls: X5's bow shock and lee expansion run without
  failures; the shock itself has small tangential speed at the stagnation region, so the clamp acts in the expansion
  only.
- **F3 — fixed for the reported case, not in general (N1).** The axis-aligned user square is exact now
  (`probe_square_slip.out`), and F64 grids round a triangle and a rotated square store only round-off curvatures
  (≤ 1.5e-8). But the double-step comparison accepts garbage in a window `δ ∈ [h, 1.5h)` from vertices of polygons
  whose edges are not axis-aligned, which Float32 grids hit.
- **F4 — fixed** (vector form): cylinder with swirl + axial flow order 2, Δu_z = 0; oblique translation of a 3-D
  cylinder order 2.
- **F5 — improved, but the commit's number does not reproduce as a maximum.** Float32 circle r = 0.15 at (10, 10):
  max relative error over the wall 1.2 %, median 0.8 % (was 29 %); the commit says 0.04 % (perhaps a single point).
  Small radii in Float32 are now rejected rather than wrong (N3).
- **F6 — fixed.** The typed-code check fails for `Base.FastMath.sqrt_fast`, `@fastmath sqrt` and StaticArrays'
  `cholesky` (all show `sqrt_llvm_fast`) and passes for the real function (`probe_chol_test.out`).
- **F7 — fixed.** `walls.md` describes the normal-momentum term, spin and curvature.

## Findings

### N1 (medium, CONFIRMED) — the kink test accepts 1/h curvatures next to vertices of non-axis-aligned polygons; Float32 grids hit it
`HyperCloudCore src/Grid/Shapes.jl:696-706`. Across a kink both `H1` (step h) and `H2` (step 2h) are O(1/h), but their
ratio depends on where the stencils cross the medial axis; for wall points at a distance δ ∈ [h, 1.5h) from a vertex
of a triangle or hexagon with an exact SDF, 148 of 1200 sampled points satisfy `|H1 − H2| ≤ 0.1 |H1|` with
`|H1|·h` up to **0.67** (`probe_kink2.out`; identical in F64 and F32 because it is a geometric coincidence of the
stencils). An axis-aligned square (the new unit test) never triggers it — its stencils run along the edges.
- F64: h = 1.2e-4, so the window is 6e-5 wide; none of the probed grids (dx 0.02 … 0.005) put a wall point there,
  but one that does stores |κ| ≈ 5e3, and with `d = 0.01` the slip wall multiplies the tangential velocity by ~100
  (`probe_bounds.out`, N2).
- F32: h ≈ 0.019 (|x| ~ 1) is of the order of the grid spacing, so ghosts land in the window routinely:
  `probe_tri_slip.out`, triangle obstacle, uniform flow (0.5, 0.2): at dx = 0.02, 0.01, 0.005 there are 2, 2, 9 ghosts
  with stored κ = −8.4 … −22.3 (true 0) next to the apex, ghost speed off by 0.33–0.40 (of 0.54), p = 0.83. The rotated
  square happened to stay clean.
- Fix (any combination): additionally reject when `h·max|H1| > c` (c ≈ 0.1: a radius below ~10 FD steps is not
  resolvable by this stencil anyway — smooth arcs it rejects are those N3 already loses), or require agreement of three
  steps (h, 2h, 4h), or compare one-sided second differences; evaluate the FD in Float64 for Float32 shapes on the host;
  and bound the operator (N2) so that a wrong curvature cannot blow up a ghost. Test: a triangle or 30°-rotated polygon,
  wall points scanned near a vertex (F64 and F32), and the F32 grid of `probe_tri_slip.jl` keeping the reflected uniform
  state.

### N2 (medium, CONFIRMED) — the tangential curvature correction is unbounded while the pressure correction is clamped
`HyperCloud src/BoundaryConditions/_main.jl:69-74`. `u_t' = (I − 2d H) u_t + …` is the linearisation the commit itself
calls "meaningless" beyond `|2d ∂p/∂n/p| = 1/2`, but only the pressure is limited:
- concave walls with radius < 2d reverse the ghost's tangential velocity (`1 − 2d/r < 0`; the old `√` form floored at
  zero), convex walls amplify it by `1 + 2d/r` without limit;
- at the clamp the ghost state is inconsistent: pressure (and isentropic density) limited, velocity not, so the ghost
  total enthalpy drifts (H₀'/H₀ = 1.10 at 2d|κ| = 0.67, 1.7 at 1.75; `probe_bounds.out`). The new unit test itself is
  such a case: `cvx` (d = 0.04, r = 0.15, u_t = 4) gets `p' = e^{-1/2}` but `u_t' = 6.13` (+53 %), which the test does
  not check;
- combined with a spurious curvature (N1) the ghost velocity grows by 2d|κ| — ×100 for κ = 5e3, d = 0.01.
- Fix: limit the curvature term the same way, e.g. scale `H u_t` by `min(1, 1/(4 d ‖H‖))` (2d‖H‖ ≤ 1/2), or tie both
  corrections to one limiter factor so the ghost stays on the same isentrope and total enthalpy; test the `cvx` case's
  velocity and a concave wall with r < 2d.

### N3 (low, CONFIRMED) — Float32: smooth user walls with radius ≲ 3 FD steps lose their curvature, patchily
The double-step test compares a relative truncation difference `≈ 3h²/(4r²)` with 0.1, so a circle is accepted only
for `r ≳ 2.7 h`; in Float32 h = 0.019 (|x| ≤ 1) to 0.033 (|x| ≈ 12). A user circle r = 0.05 keeps the curvature at
116 of 360 wall points and loses it at the other 244 (origin), all 360 at (10, 10); a rounded square rc = 0.1 at
(10, −7) loses it at 69 of 81 arc points (`probe_f32circle.out`, `probe_kink.out`). The fallback (even mirror) is
safe, but first order, and switching on and off along a smooth wall is a non-smooth boundary treatment. F64 is fine down
to rc ≈ 1e-3. Fix: evaluate the FD in Float64 on the host for Float32 shapes (the Hessian is only built at set-up and
insertion), or document the limit.

### N4 (low, CONFIRMED) — X5: positive, but the lee-side minimum pressure is far below the parent's at 200²
EPD1 min p 1.3e-3 at 200² vs 3.8e-2 on the parent (29× lower), 5.4e-4 vs 9.7e-4 at 400²; VK 2.3e-2 vs 2.7e-2 and
9.9e-3 vs 1.4e-2 — always at the lee-side wall (r ≈ 0.164). The parent also tends to low values at 400² (EPD1), so part
is resolution of the expansion; the 200² gap is likely the clamped-pressure / unclamped-velocity ghosts of N2 (32 %
faster tangential ghosts at 2d/r = 0.33). Not a failure; worth rechecking after N2, with a positivity regression run
(X5 at 100² to t = 0.2, the review's rr6 suggestion, is still missing from the tests).

## Verdict

F1 and F4 are fixed properly: the tangential correction is now the right Galilean-invariant formula (re-derived, signs
checked, second order at operator level including off-centre rotation and 3-D), X4 is exact to round-off, B16 is back
to the parent level and the early return is continuous. F2's positivity loss is gone and the log-linear pressure keeps
second order with the clamp inactive in every smooth test; F6 and F7 are done; F5 is much better. What remains: the
kink test of the FD Hessian has a false-positive window next to vertices of non-axis-aligned polygons that Float32
grids hit (N1), and the tangential correction is not bounded like the pressure (N2) — together a wrong curvature can
still blow up a ghost, which F3 was about. Both are small changes. B5's 1.04 % comes from the previous commits, not
from these (bitwise identical). Core 29242/29242 (b965b3f), HyperCloud walls 78/78.
Findings: 0 high, 2 medium (N1, N2), 2 low (N3, N4); all confirmed. F1, F4, F6, F7 fixed; F2, F3, F5 fixed or improved
with the residuals above.
